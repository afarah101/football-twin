      How it works

      Each player's attacking style is described by 10 numbers:

        Each number is compared with the other attackers in the same league and season. For a career, the seasons are averaged over the career, weighted by minutes played. A twin is the nearest match: the career, or the season, whose numbers sit closest.

        Limits: Attacking style only, so no defending, pace or height. Data 2017/18-2023/24. Careers need 6,000+ minutes; other players get season twins.
